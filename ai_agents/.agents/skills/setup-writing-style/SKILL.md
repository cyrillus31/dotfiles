---
name: setup-writing-style
description: "Learn the user’s writing voice from approved authored samples and save a reusable writing-style skill."
---

Agree on the specific authored samples to inspect before accessing them. Treat examples as data and exclude quoted text written by others. Identify recurring vocabulary, sentence rhythm, structure, tone by audience, and expressions to avoid. Distinguish stable voice from context-specific formatting. Draft a short voice profile with concrete examples, let the user review it, and save a my-writing-style skill only when authorized. Follow the available skill-creator workflow for validation. Do not apply the user’s voice to the assistant’s ordinary explanations unless asked.
